What the employer carries and what you can draw on

Method. The proposal gives notch 1 and notch 12 for each rank and phase. Notches 2 to 11 are interpolated at a constant growth rate, so treat them as estimates. Allowances do not phase in; only basic salary does. “Core package” matches section 12 of the summary (basic, housing, commuter, professorial, internet, plus Book/ICT and leave allowance spread over 12 months). Tax. Net pay is an estimate for a monthly payroll: NSSF 6% up to KSh 108,000, SHIF 2.75%, Housing Levy 1.5%, pension 10% of basic (tax-deductible up to 30,000), PAYE with KSh 2,400 personal relief. DSA is treated as non-taxable. Your payslip will differ. Source quirks. The proposal lists the University Timetabling Officer responsibility allowance as 7,000 → 50,000, and some water and electricity headers look mislabelled. Check the original before quoting them.
